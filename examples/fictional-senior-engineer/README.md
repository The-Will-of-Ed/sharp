# Sample senior software engineer

The current sample is Roran StrongHammer: nine years of professional experience, six employment records including two earlier internships, 35 work bullets, 14 side projects, two education records with coursework, 49 skills, and four approved summary options. The profile and employment claims are invented, including the IBM role. Contact and profile links are illustrative, not verified contact channels.

`master.yaml` uses Sharp's JSON Resume core plus `x_resume`. Confidence is neutral (`unrated`); approval means accepted scenario wording, not external verification. Each claim points to scenario evidence. No personal candidate data was imported.

The profile has backend/full-stack, cloud, streaming and developer-tools work, plus modest independent AI projects. It has no professional ML-training, hyperscale, Rust/C++ or kernel/graphics experience. A selector must preserve those limits.

The checked-in gallery is in `samples/roran-stronghammer/`. Its PDFs reuse selections made before the two internships were added; those internships are available for future tailoring. See `docs/REAL_JOBS_BENCHMARK.md` for historical benchmark results, which used an earlier profile and are not byte-for-byte results for this current fixture.
