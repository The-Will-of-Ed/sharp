---
name: sharp-cover-letter
description: Draft and produce a Sharp cover letter grounded in career evidence and the supplied job, without generating an unsolicited resume.
---

Inspect the career and job. Choose a small number of directly relevant, defensible contributions. Write in the user's voice, preserving estimates, contribution levels and uncertainty. Do not infer motivation, personal connections, finance expertise, or credentials not supplied.

Supply the reviewed draft as letter_text with document_type=cover_letter to document_create. Use the same settings/output contract; PDF is default and Office exports are opt-in. A document draft does not approve new master claims. If requested facts are missing, obtain context or propose a sourced career update separately.

Inspect the exported preview and text. Formatting-only edits and exports reuse the run. Give full paths. If both a resume and cover letter are requested, use the separate resume workflow as well; do not invent another pair-specific engine.
